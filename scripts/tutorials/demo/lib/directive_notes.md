# Directive 2005/36/EC notes
The Directive on the recognition of professional qualifications establishes rules under which a Member State which makes access to a regulated profession dependent on possession of specific professional qualifications must recognise professional qualifications obtained in one or more other Member States. The general system applies where no automatic recognition exists. Compensation measures, such as an aptitude test or an adaptation period, may be required where training differs substantially. Recunoașterea calificărilor profesionale se bazează pe încrederea reciprocă între statele membre.
