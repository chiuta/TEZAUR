=== TEZAUR Research ===
Ask your own library from WordPress. Requires a running `tezaur serve` reachable from the web server.
Shortcodes: [tezaur_ask]  [tezaur_search]  [tezaur_ask mode="compare"]
The token is stored in WordPress options and never sent to the browser.
Answers come from a local model and can take minutes; visitors are rate limited when public use is on.
